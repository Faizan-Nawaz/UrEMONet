About UrEMONet

A multimodal deep learning system for Urdu emotion recognition — built as a Final Year Project using video, audio and text fusion on Urdu drama clips.

Multimodal Fusion

Combines video frames, speech audio and Urdu text transcriptions using cross-modal projection for accurate emotion prediction.

Deep Learning Pipeline

Powered by MTCNN + FaceNet for video, Wav2Vec2-Urdu for audio, and XLM-RoBERTa for text feature extraction.

Urdu Drama Dataset

Trained on 8,588 annotated video clips extracted from Urdu drama series, covering 5 emotion classes.

Final Year Project

Built at University of Sialkot to advance emotion recognition research for low-resource Urdu language.
